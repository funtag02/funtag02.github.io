Professional experience and personal projects in embedded systems, software, and applied AI.

LuminaSnake

LuminaSnake began as an embedded C++/FreeRTOS prototype: a compact, affordable way to bring festival-like music-reactive lighting home, without the cost or storage space of professional equipment.

The physical prototype responds well to high-frequency-only passages, but in full-spectrum playback continuous bass often masks other musical events. I moved development temporarily to Python so I can experiment with signal processing and refine the intended behavior and visual rendering more easily before returning to the embedded version.

theoOS

A learning-first bare-metal mini-RTOS written in C from scratch for the STM32H750B-DK, without HAL, CubeMX, or an RTOS library. Phase 1 (toolchain and binary foundations) is complete; Phase 2 (startup, GPIO, SysTick, and UART) is in progress.
